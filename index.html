The Scenario

"A dormant partner suddenly demands an immediate dividend payout based on an old verbal promise."

Weak Strategy (Penalty)

"The dormant partner is bad and wants a dividend from our share."

Why it fails: Keyword stuffing, no logical legal framework or synthesis of terms.

Winning Strategy (+$150,000 Swing)

"According to our articles of association, the dormant partner has no legal standing to demand a dividend. Their proportion of the issued share capital does not grant voting rights or immediate returns unless explicitly authorized by the board of directors."

Why it wins: Perfect contextual synthesis of corporate terms to protect the company legally.
